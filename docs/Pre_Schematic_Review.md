## Verification basis and checks

I checked all four selection files, all five circuit documents including loop status, MCU/resource/clock/default tables, interfaces, command/telemetry contracts, host source/tests, calculations, local library targets, reference metadata, workflows, mechanical/fabrication plans and document cross-links. Semiconductor pin/package conclusions use manufacturer documents; stock-library existence and repository audits have narrower scope.

My bounded ngspice DC run reproduced the independent equations: approximately 9.990234 V at the ADC for a 10 V input, 3.997871 V for a 20 mA sense input, and 0.648825 V at the MCU diagnostic ADC for the modeled 0.5 A output. Filter poles were approximately 143.8 Hz and 131.9 Hz. These are illustrative fixed-resistance, biased-input and finite-gain ideal-amplifier operating points; they do not simulate converter dynamics, IC fault recovery, stability, temperature or a whole board. [Model and check instructions](calcs/README.md).

The reproducible checks are the [repository audit](../scripts/audit_project.py), [selection scope audit](../scripts/audit_component_selections.py), [capture availability audit](../scripts/audit_capture_readiness.py), four [calculation scripts](calcs/README.md), [host tests](../tools/diagnostics/tests/test_diagnostics.py) and explicitly simulated CLI identity/snapshot examples. An asset report can pass its structural checks while reporting an open gate.

I also archived six [manufacturer capacitor characteristic exports](../references/capacitors/README.md) for the selected GRM31CR71E106KA12L. At 1 kHz/0.01 Vrms, its sampled typical capacitance over 0–50 °C is about 5.68–6.66 µF at 5.25 V and 6.17–7.55 µF at 3.4 V. These model curves support screening but do not establish production tolerance, aging or guaranteed minima; all capacitor gates remain open. No numeric curve result is substituted for the other selected parts.
