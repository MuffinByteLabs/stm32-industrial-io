# Independent disposition of the external review — October 6, 2026

**The review identified real documentation and selection defects. I corrected the buffer outputs, fitted power connector, reset supervisor/oscillator selection and digital-regulator package. I also adopted a conditional power-good startup sequence, reserved digital-input protection sites and reduced custom connector work using exact stock candidates. Every selected board feature remains.**

The suggested wider 5 V health window and protection deletions are not approved replacements. The current design still needs the capacitor, power sequencing, library, sourcing, native implementation and measured qualification work described below. This record supersedes affected component values and readiness counts in the [October 4 review](Pre_Schematic_Review.md); that review remains the historical record of its own changes. The earlier review missed the buffer map, power-connector allocation and reset release mismatch.

## Evidence and scope

I read the full supplied [external report](../references/reviews/external_pre_capture_report_2026-10-05.txt), reviewed its calculation source before execution, reran its arithmetic, inspected the active specifications and selections, compared the challenged circuits with manufacturer tables and checked installed KiCad assets. The original [calculation script](../references/reviews/independent_verification_calcs_2026-10-06.py) is preserved byte-for-byte; its [provenance](../references/reviews/external_review_2026-10-06.json) identifies both the downloaded script and archived original report. The public text source neutralizes only the local workspace path; original bytes are preserved locally outside the repository, and historical relative links assume a different location; active drawing instructions are in this project's [capture package](Schematic_Capture.md).

The script uses only arithmetic, `math`, `json` and standard output. Static inspection found no filesystem writes, process execution or network operations before it was run. Reproducing a calculation does not establish the assumptions behind it. It remains reference material, separate from the active project calculations.

**High confidence** below means a manufacturer pin/package/table, installed asset or directly inspected project fact establishes the conclusion. **Conditional** means the arithmetic is reproducible but depends on explicitly unverified leakage, converter, thermal or physical assumptions. No native schematic/PCB exists yet; ERC, DRC, layout EMC, extracted thermal analysis and whole-board fault simulation are unavailable at this stage. No assembled-board rating or standards compliance is established.

The TPS62160 feedback-current maximum of 400 nA is tested at FB=1.2 V, whereas regulation uses about 0.8 V. The signed allowance used by the rail screen is deliberately conservative; it is not a guaranteed bidirectional limit at the operating point. Line, load and ripple effects still need closure. TPS3808 hysteresis is 1% typical and 2.5% maximum; the release screen includes that maximum plus threshold tolerance.

STM32's full specified USB behavior requires 3.0–3.6 V. G30 reset release is not a precise 3.0 V USB undervoltage detector. I added independent digital-rail collapse, USB detach/recovery and incomplete-calibration-write tests to [service instructions](circuits/Control_Service.md), [firmware requirements](../firmware/README.md) and [validation](Validation.md). The normal digital-rail screen exceeds 3.0 V, but brownout operation is still a qualification gate. [STM32 DS12288 Table 92](../references/datasheets/STM32G474VE.pdf).

### The 5 V window is tighter than the external trip-only calculation suggests

The updated [support calculation](calcs/support_checks.py) includes resistor drift, ADC load, the AVDD filter and maximum TPS3702 hysteresis. For the strict window, worst-case reentry bounds are 4.881600 V and 5.111600 V. The main rail's reference/divider DC screen including resistor drift is 4.912216–5.088216 V; ADC AVDD can be 4.900083 V after the series resistor. The resulting DC reentry margins are only **18.483 mV and 23.384 mV**. Against the broader 4.90–5.10 V design target, the corresponding margins are **6.268 mV and 11.600 mV** after filter allowance.

I do not label this margin acceptable without a load-step/ripple budget. Capture must preserve an explicit gate for normal startup/recovery, relay switching, both isolated converters, acquisition load and the complete commanded-output fixture. If it fails, revise regulation/filtering/monitor allocation as a coordinated change while preserving the hardware permission and ADC validity contracts.

## R1–R5: proposed component deletions

| Proposal | Why it is not adopted in this baseline |
| --- | --- |
| R1: combine digital and field logic rails | A plausible architecture alternative, but two same-ground rails can still start, collapse or fail differently. Common ancestry does not make their powered-off crossings unnecessary. A real merger needs loading, noise, supervision and failure-scope review; removing buffers while retaining separate rails is not the proposal. It does not address the independent positive protection-rail sequence. |
| R2: delete TMUX7462F, +15 V and VFP rails | ADS8684A permits ±20 V input stress under stated AVDD conditions, including a <30 kΩ AVDD-to-ground path; the retained 10 kΩ bleeder is relevant. This makes an alternative worth studying, but does not prove continuous ±30 V terminal miswires, transient clamps or complete power-off impedances. Removing the switches also removes independent input-fault indications and requires replacement data-validity/recovery behavior. I did not claim identical protection or retire SEQ-01 from paper arithmetic. [ADS8684A p5/absolute-maximum note](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads8684a.pdf). |
| R3: delete the buffered, referenced and switched current-diagnostic path | **Rejected as presented.** Current limited to a fraction of a milliamp can still charge an unpowered rail unless a guaranteed sink exists. TPS709 reverse protection is not a sink. The suggested clamp current is not proof of MCU pin safety or absence of backpower; the existing TMUX1511 power-off protection remains. The proposed chain reads about 1.350 V at 0.5 A versus 0.6488 V now, approximately **2.081×**, so “−1.7% gain change” describes only the CS burden effect, not total conversion gain. ADC range, fault separation and calibration would need redesign. No assertion is made that the calculated external diode current alone proves an internal MCU injection-limit violation. |
| R4: ISOW1412/ISOW1044 integrated isolated power | Possible alternatives, not drop-ins. ISOW1412 is full duplex and needs explicit Y→A/Z→B wiring for a half-duplex port; its bus absolute range is −12/+15 V. The current bidirectional protection's stated −14.4 V clamp cannot transfer unchanged. ISOW1044 changes receiver common-mode range from ±30 to ±12 V and bus-fault rating from ±70 to ±58 V. Both devices still need bulk/bypass capacitance, ferrites and specified layout; two separate secondary islands can remain independent, but capacitor/startup/EMC gates are not eliminated. [ISOW1412](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/isow1412.pdf), [ISOW1044](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/isow1044.pdf), [ISO1042](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/iso1042.pdf). |
| R5: tie health outputs to one wired-AND | Replaces distinct reset, supply-enable and actuator-permission scopes with a new circuit, while losing separate fault visibility. The described combination omits the watchdog-disabled state represented by ENOUT. Adding ENOUT to the existing watchdog WDO→supervisor MR path could instead create startup reset lock. All open-drain/supply/current states and the entire truth table must be redesigned. The retained asynchronous ARM clearing, all six output gates and fresh ARM after recovery remain. |

These proposals may preserve headline connector counts while changing protection, supervision or diagnosis. Consequently, the external claim of deleting 25–37 active parts “without dropping any feature” is not verified. No replacement design, netlist or fault qualification was supplied to establish it.

## Calculation and standards claims that were not adopted

- The external script's `tick_us` field calculates a frequency in MHz, not a tick period. Both happen to equal 1 at the selected 1 MHz timer. Period is `1e6 / (144e6 / 144)` microseconds.
- ADS8684A drift figures used by the script are maximum-table entries, but an RSS total still assumes an independence/statistical model. Calibration-source accuracy, temperature behavior, TVS leakage change and shunt heating assumptions are not measured guarantees. The existing calibrated accuracy targets stay open.
- The script's buck-bank shorthand uses three 22 µF parts where the selected field buck has four. Its bottom-up service and startup estimates do not supersede the 6 W ceiling, 5.75 W reservations or complete converter-tree charging gate. There is no basis for “everything else has margin”: low-line input power, the positive auxiliary reservation and 5 V reentry remain narrow.
- `spice_dc_check.py --library` identifies the **ngspice solver DLL**, not a collection of vendor IC models. The project's bounded ideal DC testbench can run with the installed DLL; it does not simulate real IC faults or startup.
- Murata CSVs are typical manufacturer characteristic/model exports, not measured-board curves or guaranteed minimum capacitance. All 14 numeric banks, two isolated-port gates and the parent hold-up evidence remain open.
- Nominal DI thresholds and output voltage-drop arithmetic do not establish IEC 61131-2 compliance. TVS presence does not establish ESD/EFT/surge immunity, and ≤30 V nominal operation alone does not establish a SELV system or a particular safety classification. The existing finite DC fixtures and isolation targets remain separate from certification claims. No mandatory clock or fabrication rule is inferred from an unsourced standards summary.

The attached instructions to commit/tag, delete files and create the native project were treated as review recommendations, not user requests. The original review/source files remain preserved locally; public copies omit local computer paths and record the changed hashes; this pass changed the engineering package and its verification evidence. I did not commit, tag, remove assets, create a schematic, order parts or reserve inventory.
