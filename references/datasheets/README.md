## Revision and local-cache limits

I checked ST's current datasheet and errata guidance. DS12288 Rev6, November2021, remains the current listed STM32G474 family datasheet; an old publication date does not by itself mean obsolescence. My local copy is a manufacturer-authored DigiKey mirror, and my provisional LQFP100 peripheral allocation still needs physical pin/CubeMX confirmation.

I checked the G5Q family's current manufacturer production status and April2026 document listing. The local G5Q PDF remains the official June2021 archive because the current direct download was blocked. I will check the regional ordering code, coil/contact tables, drawing and approvals against the current document before relay selection freeze.

I checked the current Littelfuse SMCJ datasheet, JC.02/27/25 v.2, online. Where its local replacement download remains blocked, I retain the four-page historical manufacturer-authored catalog excerpt for provenance only. Protection calculations use the current manufacturer row, not the 2005-metadata archive. ADG5401F primary specifications and pin/control behavior were also reviewed online; its local download status is explicit in the index and manifest.

I replaced the unused UCC12050 candidate reference with UCC33421-Q1 for the revised isolated-port power budget, and selected the dual OPA2197 from the existing OPAx197 family document. I retain manufacturer attribution for every PDF.
