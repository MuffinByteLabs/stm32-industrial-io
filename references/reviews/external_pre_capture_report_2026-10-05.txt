## 1. Verdict

**The engineering package is very strong, but five items need resolving before you start the schematic.**

The documentation, traceability and arithmetic are far more thorough than is typical at this stage. Every number I recomputed matches the project's own value within rounding (§6). The project audits pass (40 Markdown files, 396 local links, 41 datasheet identities, 162 selection groups / 140 order codes, 0 failures), and all 30 host-tool unit tests pass.

The five items to fix first (§2):

- a datasheet pin-map error that would cross-wire 10 logic packages
- a missing main power connector
- two reset/health supervisor windows with too little worst-case margin
- five key ICs that had no authorized-distributor stock in my snapshot

After those, the remaining gate is the project's own LIB-01: 31 selection groups still need symbols or footprints.

As a recommendation rather than a defect: the controller is much more complex than is typical for this class of board. It has 64 ICs, 29 of them single- or dual-gate logic, plus 7 discrete transistors, across 512 placements. The optional simplifications in §4 would remove roughly 25–37 active parts without dropping anything in `Scope.md`. They would also close the project's highest-risk open gate (SEQ-01) and make hand assembly much easier.

## 2. Must fix before you start the schematic

### B1. SN74LVC2G125 output pins are swapped in the documentation (10 packages)

The datasheet ([SN74LVC2G125.pdf](../references/datasheets/SN74LVC2G125.pdf), SCES204Q, Pin Functions) gives this DCU pin map:

| Pin | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Function | 1OE | 1A | **2Y** | GND | 2A | **1Y** | 2OE | VCC |

The project documents it the other way round:

- [Control_Service.md](circuits/Control_Service.md), line 133: `1 OE1_N; 2 A1; 3 Y1; 4 GND; 5 A2; 6 Y2; 7 OE2_N; 8 VCC`. This swaps Y1 and Y2.
- [Loop_Status.md](circuits/Loop_Status.md), pin table: `3 1Y → LOOP1_BAD_MCU`, `6 2Y → LOOP2_BAD_MCU`. Also swapped.
- `docs/components/control_service.json`, `LS_QUALIFY` evidence text: same wording.

**Impact.** If the project symbol is drawn from these tables, every LVC2G125 cross-wires its two channels. The 9 domain-crossing buffers would put each signal on the other channel's net, and loop status 1 and 2 would be reported swapped. The SN74LVC2G08 table in the same file is correct (Y1 = 7, Y2 = 3), so the error is local to this part.

**Fix.**

- Correct both tables to `2 1A → 6 1Y` and `5 2A → 3 2Y`.
- In Loop_Status.md: `6 1Y → LOOP1_BAD_MCU (PD8)` and `3 2Y → LOOP2_BAD_MCU (PD9)`.
- Alternatively, use KiCad's stock `74xGxx:74LVC2G125` symbol after checking its pin numbers against the datasheet.

### B2. The main DC input connector is not in the fitted BOM

`power.json` contains no connector. The 2-position MSTB header and plug (1759017 / 1757019) appear only as catalogue entries with quantity 0 in `field_io.json`. [Field_IO.md](circuits/Field_IO.md), line 192, says the power sheet "owns" their fitted quantity, but the power sheet never takes it. As a result, the 9–30 V, 3 A input has no connector.

**Fix.** Add a fitted header and plug to `power.json`. I recommend **MSTB 2,5/2-G-5,08 (1759017) + MSTB 2,5/2-ST-5,08 (1757019)**:

- It has a different pin count from the 3-position relay plugs and 4-position output plugs, so the 24 V supply cannot be pushed into a relay or output connector.
- MSTB 2,5 is rated well above the 5 A fuse.

If you want FE on the same plug (see S4), use a coded 3-position part rather than the uncoded 1759020 the relays use, for the same mis-mating reason.

### B3. TPS3808G33 reset release can sit above the worst-case 3.3 V digital rail

| Quantity | Value | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TPS3808G33 threshold, VIT | 3.07 V ±1.5% (−40…125 °C) | [TPS3808.pdf](../references/datasheets/TPS3808.pdf), electrical characteristics |
| Hysteresis, fixed versions | 1% typical, **2.5% maximum** | same table |
| **Reset release, worst case** | 3.07 × 1.015 × 1.025 = **3.194 V** | |
| 3V3_DIG nominal (TPS62160, 30.9 kΩ + 301 Ω over 10 kΩ, VREF 0.8 V) | 3.296 V | |
| TPS62160 initial accuracy | −3% / +3% (PWM); **−3.5% / +4% (power-save mode, i.e. light load at power-up)** | [TPS62160.pdf](../references/datasheets/TPS62160.pdf) |
| FB leakage error (400 nA × 31.2 kΩ) | 12.5 mV | |
| **3V3_DIG worst-case minimum** | **3.168 V** (PSM) / 3.185 V (PWM) | |
| **Margin** | **−26 mV** (PSM) / −9 mV (PWM) | |
| TPS70933 rails (±1%, line ≤ 10 mV, load ≤ 50 mV) minimum | 3.207 V → **+13 mV** | [TPS709.pdf](../references/datasheets/TPS709.pdf) |

A typical pair has about 200 mV of margin, so a bench prototype will probably start. A worst-case pair, however, can hold `RESET_OK` low indefinitely, or chatter on load steps. Industry practice is to design reset thresholds to worst case.

**Fix (recommended):** change all three supervisors to **TPS3808G30DBVR**. VIT is 2.79 V, so the worst-case release is 2.903 V. That gives +266 mV against the 3V3_DIG minimum and +304 mV against the LDO minimum. The footprint and pins are the same.

Check every device on those rails down to the 2.748 V minimum trip point:

| Device | Status |
| --- | --- |
| STM32G474 (1.71–3.6 V) | OK |
| 24LC64 (2.5–5.5 V) | OK |
| LVC/AUP logic | OK |
| ISO1212 VCC1 (2.25–5.5 V) | OK |
| ISO1410 and ISO1042 VCC1 | OK |
| ADS8684A DVDD (specified performance from 2.7 V) | OK |
| TMUX1511 | OK |
| SiT8008 with the "33" supply code (2.97–3.63 V) | **Not OK.** Change the code to **"XX" (2.25–3.63 V)**: `SiT8008BI-22-XXS-8.000000E`. The datasheet lists both options (Vdd_XX, Vdd_YY). |

*Alternative:* raise 3V3_DIG with a 32.4 kΩ top resistor. That gives 3.392 V nominal, 3.260 V minimum and 3.541 V maximum. It leaves only about 60 mV below the STM32's 3.6 V limit, so I don't prefer it.

### B4. ADC5_OK gates actuator permission and isolated power on a ±4% window with about 60 mV of margin

| Quantity | Value |
| --- | --- |
| TPS3702CX50 with SET high: nominal UV / OV | 4.80 V / 5.20 V (±4%), accuracy ±0.9% ([TPS3702.pdf](../references/datasheets/TPS3702.pdf)) |
| Worst-case UV trip / OV trip | ≤ 4.843 V / ≥ 5.153 V |
| ADC_AVDD DC range (LMR38020 VREF ±1.5%, 0.1% divider, 11.5 mA through the 1 Ω filter) | 4.906–5.083 V |
| **DC margin** | **62 mV low / 70 mV high**, before ripple, load steps, drift and comparator response |

That window drives `FIELD5V_VALID = FIELD_OK AND ADC5_OK`, and through it:

- start-up of both UCC33421 converters
- `HEALTH_READY`, then `CLR_N`, then the asynchronous clear of the ARM latch

**Failure scenario.** A 5 V load step (two relay coils at 99 mA each, UCC33421 start-up, CAN dominant current) dips the rail by more than 62 mV. That clears permission, and a fresh ARM is required by design. It also shuts down both isolated converters. The resulting load release overshoots toward a 70 mV OV margin. The outcome is nuisance disarms in the field and a possible hiccup loop.

**Fix (no new BOM line, just quantity 1 → 2).**

1. Keep the existing TPS3702CX50 with SET high on ADC_AVDD, but route it only to the MCU as an **ADC data-valid** flag. The tight window is right for that job, because it brackets the ADS8684A's 4.75–5.25 V AVDD range.
2. Add a **second TPS3702CX50DDCR with SET tied to GND**, which gives the ±9% band (TPS3702 datasheet SBVS251A §6.3.3: "when VSET ≤ VIL(SET) … ±9%"). Put it on 5V_FIELD and use it for `FIELD5V_VALID` and `HEALTH_READY`.
   - Worst-case trips: 4.509–4.591 V and 5.401–5.499 V.
   - Margin: **326 mV / 318 mV**.
   - The window stays inside the UCC33421's 4.5–5.5 V input range, and the relays (75% must-operate = 3.75 V) are unaffected.
3. During bring-up, measure the LMR38020 response to a 0.5 A step. The output is 3 × 22 µF X7R, about 40 µF effective at 5 V.

## 3. Should fix (during capture, before layout or release)

### S1. The digital inputs have no surge protection

The current input network is RTHR = 100 Ω MELF, RSENSE = 562 Ω, CIN = 1 nF, with no TVS. TI's ISO1212 datasheet ([ISO1212.pdf](../references/datasheets/ISO1212.pdf), §8.2.1.2.5, Table 8-1) recommends this for Type 3 inputs:

- **RTHR = 1 kΩ with CIN = 10 nF** for ±1 kV line-to-PE and line-to-line surge, or 330 nF for ±1 kV line-to-FGND.
- For a smaller RTHR, which this design needs to keep VIH ≤ 9 V, TI says to use TVS diodes or varistors instead (Figure 8-10). TI names VCAN26A2-03S, EZJ-P0V420WM and GSOT36C.

IEC 61131-2 requires IEC 61000-4-5 surge immunity on DC I/O.

**Fix.** Add one bidirectional TVS per input, from IN to FGND/DI_COM. Use **GSOT36C** (TI-listed, 36 V standoff) or **SMBJ33CA** (SMB, easy to hand-solder).

- Don't use VCAN26A2-03S: its 26 V standoff is below your 30 V input maximum.
- Keep RTHR at 100 Ω, so the 8.79 V / 6.72 V thresholds don't change.
- TI also recommends about 500 pF from FGND to PE. That ties into S4.

### S2. The 5 V buck runs while the eFuse is still ramping

LMR38020 EN is tied to VFIELD, and TPS2663 PGOOD is only a test point. The buck therefore starts at about 4 V on the eFuse output and loads the eFuse FET while the FET is still dropping most of the input voltage.

Upper-bound estimate, assuming 7.5 W input during the 500 V/s ramp:

| Input | Energy into the eFuse FET (buck running) | Energy if the buck waits for PGOOD |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 24 V | 0.086 J capacitor charge + up to 0.33 J (about 8.7 W average over 48 ms) | about 1.8 W average |
| 30 V | 0.135 J + 0.50 J (10.5 W over 60 ms) | about 2.2 W average |

The real start-up load is smaller, because the isolated converters and relays are gated off. It still includes the 680 µF precharge (S3).

**Fix.**

1. Move the **PGOOD pull-up from 3V3_FIELD_LOGIC (10 kΩ) to VFIELD (100 kΩ)** and connect PGOOD to LMR38020 EN. This is within ratings: PGOOD is rated 67 V absolute maximum and 60 V recommended; LMR38020 EN is rated to VIN + 0.3 V, with a 1.1–1.4 V rising threshold.
2. Re-scale PGTH from 604 k / 100 k (now 8.28–8.62 V rising, 7.67–8.10 V falling, which leaves only about 0.3 V above the 8.4 V minimum VFIELD) to about **475 k / 100 k (6.76–7.04 V rising, 6.27–6.61 V falling)**. That way normal dips don't drop the 5 V rail. `FIELD_OK` remains the operating-validity monitor.
3. PGOOD then sits at VFIELD level, so it can no longer go to an MCU pin. Nothing is lost: it was only a test point. The MCU keeps `INPUT_FAULT_N` (FLT) on PD10 and FIELD_OK for input status.

### S3. The 680 µF hold-up capacitor precharges through the boost inductor during the 5 V soft-start

The TPS61040's inductor-plus-diode path is a direct DC path from 5 V to the 15 V bulk. During the LMR38020's 4 ms soft-start, the bulk draws 680 µF × 1.2 × 5 V / 4 ms ≈ **1.0 A**. The 15 µH / 680 µF LC is essentially undamped (Z₀ ≈ 0.15 Ω). This is within the buck's 2 A rating, but it is real stress and it belongs to the project's own SEQ-01 gate.

**Fix:** adopt R2, which deletes the whole branch. Otherwise, add a series resistor or NTC in the boost feed and capture the waveform during bring-up.

### S4. There is no functional-earth (FE) or chassis path

The board has five ground references and no earth path:

- isolated DI FGND/DI_COM
- RS485_REF
- CAN_REF
- MAIN_GND
- the USB shield

[Interfaces.md](Interfaces.md) leaves shield bonding to the installer. IEC 61000-4-2/-4/-5 testing, and real installations, need a defined discharge path for each isolated domain.

**Industry-standard fix:**

- Add an FE terminal: a screw or mounting-hole lug, or the third pin of a coded input plug.
- Add footprints, DNP by default, for **1 MΩ ∥ 4.7 nF / 2 kV (or Y2)** from each isolated reference and MAIN_GND to FE.
- Add an optional USB-shield RC (1 MΩ ∥ 4.7 nF).
- Add TI's 500 pF from FGND to PE for the ISO1212.

The installer can then choose the bonding without a board spin.

### S5. Several connectors are identical and can be cross-mated

The board has identical headers in three groups:

- 4 × MC 1,5/3-G-3,5 (DI1, DI2, RS-485, CAN)
- 2 × MSTB 3-position (relays)
- 2 × MSTB 4-position (DO)

An RS-485 plug fits a DI header, for example. The usual practice is to fit Phoenix's coding profiles or sections for the MC and MSTB series (order the matching header and plug coding parts) or to use different plug colours, plus clear silkscreen legends.

### S6. Thermal plan

The board dissipates about **5.8 W at 24 V and full load**.

| Source | Dissipation |
| --- | --- |
| Series blocking diodes | 4 × 0.29 W |
| Two UCC33421 | 1.21 W |
| LMR38020 and inductor | 0.82 W |
| Relay coils | 0.79 W |
| TPS4H160 | 0.47 W at 24 V (0.52 W hot worst case, +17 °C at the JEDEC 32.7 °C/W rating) |
| eFuse, FET and fuse | 0.46 W |

In a sealed enclosure, this sets the internal temperature rise. Give each SMA diode about 100 mm² of copper. Stitch thermal vias under every exposed pad (TPS2663, LMR38020, TPS4H160, TPS7A16). Then estimate the enclosure ΔT before you commit to the 0–50 °C rating.

### S9. Optional: CAN FD clock

FDCAN at 48 MHz works: 96 time quanta at 500 kbit/s and 24 at 2 Mbit/s. However, CiA 601-3 recommends 20, 40 or 80 MHz for CAN FD interoperability. A clean alternative:

- 8 MHz HSE ÷ 2 × 80 = 320 MHz VCO
- SYSCLK 160 MHz (PLLR ÷ 2)
- FDCAN 80 MHz (PLLQ ÷ 4)
- USB from HSI48, trimmed by CRS from USB SOF

All of these are within the STM32G4 PLL limits.

## 4. Recommended simplifications (optional, industry-typical)

`Architecture_Review.md` currently says "Retain the complete documented architecture". Each item below is therefore a proposal that would need those documents updated. None of them removes a feature listed in `Scope.md`.

### R1. Merge 3V3_DIG and 3V3_FIELD_LOGIC into one 3.3 V logic rail

Both rails come from 5V_FIELD. USB cannot power the board: it is self-powered and only senses VBUS. So the state the domain-crossing buffers guard against, one logic rail up while the other is down, exists only for milliseconds during power ramps.

- **Remove:** 9 × SN74LVC2G125 (keep the loop-status qualifier), 2 × MMBT3904 OE drivers, 1 × TPS70933, 1 × TPS3808 (FIELD3V3_OK), and their pull resistors and decoupling.
- **Keep:** the 3V3_MCU_ANA LDO, and Schmitt conditioning on slow open-drain or RC signals.
- **Effect:** about 13 fewer active parts. ISO1212, ISO1410 and ISO1042 VCC1 and ADS8684A DVDD move to 3V3_DIG, adding about 11 mA to a 1 A buck.

### R2. Remove the TMUX7462F protection path and the +15 V / VFP rails

- **Current inputs.** TPS26611 is already a ±50 V-rated loop protector (±55 V absolute maximum). Its +Vs range is 2.25–30 V. Powered from 5 V, its output over-voltage threshold becomes +Vs + 0.05 to +Vs + 0.30 V ([TPS2661.pdf](../references/datasheets/TPS2661.pdf)). That is well inside the ADS8684A's ±20 V input protection, and still 0.16 V above the 4.805 V shunt voltage at 24 mA. NAMUR NE43 over-range readings to 24 mA are preserved.
- **Voltage inputs.** The existing 1 kΩ 2512 + SMCJ11CA already clamp a ±30 V miswire to about 12–14 V at the ADC pin. The project's own analysis puts 0.32 W in the resistor. The ADS8684A rates its inputs at **±20 V when AVDD is at 5 V or has a < 30 kΩ path to ground** ([ADS8684A.pdf](../references/datasheets/ADS8684A.pdf), absolute-maximum note 2). The 10 kΩ AVDD bleeder provides that path, so the rating holds when the board is unpowered.
- **Remove:** 2 × TMUX7462F, 2 × TPS7A1601, TPS61040, XAL4040, PMEG4010CEJ, the 680 µF/25 V capacitor, 1 × TPS3700 (ANA15_OK), and about 20–25 passives.
- **Retires:** **SEQ-01** (high/high), the 15.5 V branch's 4.2 mW reserve in PWR-01/PRE-11, and S3. It also takes the switch's on-resistance and leakage out of the signal path, which improves the error budget.

### R3. Replace the DO current-diagnostic chain with TI's reference arrangement

- **Today:** CS → 1.21 kΩ ∥ 56 k/68 k → OPA2320 follower → 1 k/1.5 k → BAS70-04 to an LM4040 2.5 V clamp → TMUX1511 power-off switch → ADC.
- **Proposed:** CS (1.21 kΩ) → **22 k / 47 k divider (×0.681, −1.7% gain change, calibrated out)** → 10 nF → PB1 / ADC1_IN12, with **one BAS70-04 (already in the BOM) clamping to 3V3_MCU_ANA and GND**.
  - The 0.809 A maximum current limit reads 2.18 V.
  - The minimum 4.5 V fault level reads ≥ 3.07 V, so the two are distinguishable.
  - A 6.5 V fault pushes only 0.13 mA into the clamp.
- **Unpowered MCU:** the 22 kΩ series resistor and the clamp limit the current into an unpowered MCU to about 0.3 mA, even at the 6.5 V fault level. That is too little to back-power the rail, so the power-off switch isn't needed.
- **Keep DIAG_EN as it is now.** If DIAG_EN is low, both CS *and* FAULT go high-impedance (protection stays active; TPS4H160-Q1 datasheet Table 7-1), and the permission logic relies on FAULT.
- **Remove:** OPA2320, LM4040, TMUX1511, 1 × BAS70-04, and about 8 passives.

### R4. Isolated transceivers with integrated power (also fixes three B5 parts)

- Replace **ISO1410 + UCC33421 with ISOW1412**: RS-485, 500 kbit/s, integrated DC/DC, 20-pin SOIC (DFM, 12.83 × 10.3 mm).
- Replace **ISO1042 + UCC33421 with ISOW1044**: CAN FD to 5 Mbit/s, ±58 V bus-fault protection, 20 mA spare isolated output, VDD 4.5–5.5 V, VIO 1.71–5.5 V.

This removes both UCC33421s and about 14 support parts (EN/SEL resistors, 15 nF, 10 µF and 22 µF capacitors, BLM21 beads). If the fail-safe bias network is fitted (about 9 mA), confirm the ISOW1412 can supply it from its isolated output.

### R5. Wired-AND health signals (larger change; consider it after R1–R2)

Most health sources are open-drain: TPS3808 RESET, TPS3700, TPS3702, TPS3431 WDO and TPS4H160 FAULT. Tie them to one pulled-up `HEALTH_READY` node, follow it with a single Schmitt buffer, and keep the LVC1G74 ARM latch and the per-output PERMISSION AND gates. The MCU can work out which rail failed from its own ADC readings.

- **Saving:** potentially about 8 of the 11 LVC2G08 and 3 of the 4 LVC2G17.
- **Cost:** the documented truth tables must be re-derived, and you lose per-signal hardware visibility.

### 5.5 Hand-assembly summary

- **Hard packages and their alternatives:**

  - TPS26632RGER → **TPS26633PWPR** (optional)

- **22 VSSOP-8 (0.5 mm) logic packages**: 11 LVC2G08, 10 LVC2G125 and 1 LVC1G74. R1 and R5 would remove most of them.
- **Recommended kit:**
  - a stainless stencil, or syringe paste, plus a hot plate or preheater for the leadless and exposed-pad parts
  - a fine conical or hoof tip with flux for drag-soldering the LQFP and TSSOP parts
  - hot air for rework
- **Moisture sensitivity:** bake QFN, SON and LQFP parts per J-STD-033 if the bags have been open beyond their MSL floor life.
